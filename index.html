🎯 Step 1

When do we use it?

The Present Simple talks about three big things:

🕐

Daily Routines

Things you do every single day — like clockwork!

Example

“I brush my teeth every morning.”

🔁

Habits

Things you do again and again — on Sundays, over and over!

Example

“She rides her bike on Sundays.”

🌍

True Facts

Things that are always true — for everyone, everywhere!

Example

“The sun rises in the east.”

🪄 Step 3

The Magic S! ✨

When the “who” is He, She or It — the verb grows a shiny tail!

He · She · It

+ verb + S

One person or one thing? The verb gets bigger!

He play ➜ plays

🚨 Watch out for -ES!

After ch · sh · s · x · o → add ES

  • watch ➜ watches
  • go ➜ goes
  • pass ➜ passes

📊 Step 4

How often? 🤔

These special words tell us how often — and they love the Present Simple!

😄 Always100%

“I always brush my teeth before bed.”

🙂 Usually80%

“I usually walk to school.”

🤔 Sometimes50%

“I sometimes eat pizza for lunch.”

🚫 Never0%

“I never forget my homework.”

every day every morning on Mondays once a week twice a month every weekend

💡 These time words usually sit at the start or the end of the sentence!
